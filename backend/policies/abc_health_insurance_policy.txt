1. Scope of Coverage

This policy provides coverage for medically necessary hospitalization expenses incurred due to illness or accidental injury, subject to the terms, conditions, exclusions, and limits specified herein.

Coverage applies only if hospitalization occurs in a network or registered hospital and is certified by a registered medical practitioner.

2. Covered Benefits

The policy covers the following expenses incurred during hospitalization:

Room rent, nursing expenses, and boarding charges

Doctor’s consultation fees

Charges for diagnostic tests, laboratory investigations, and imaging

Cost of medicines and consumables prescribed during hospitalization

Operation theatre charges and surgical expenses

ICU charges where medically necessary

3. Hospitalization Requirement

Hospitalization must be for a minimum continuous period of 24 hours.
Day-care procedures requiring less than 24 hours of hospitalization are covered only if listed explicitly in Annexure A of this policy.

4. Sum Insured and Sub-limits

The maximum liability of the company under this policy shall not exceed the Sum Insured as stated in the policy schedule.

Sub-limits may apply to:

Room rent: Maximum ₹5,000 per day

ICU charges: Maximum ₹10,000 per day

Doctor consultation: As per reasonable and customary charges

5. Waiting Periods
5.1 Initial Waiting Period

All illnesses (except accidents) are subject to a 30-day waiting period from the policy commencement date.

5.2 Specific Disease Waiting Period

The following illnesses are subject to a 24-month waiting period:

Cataract

Hernia

Gall bladder stones

Joint replacement surgeries

5.3 Pre-existing Diseases

Any condition, ailment, injury, or disease diagnosed or treated prior to policy inception shall be considered pre-existing and shall be covered only after a 36-month continuous coverage period.

6. Exclusions

The company shall not be liable to make any payment for claims arising from or related to:

Treatment for obesity, cosmetic or aesthetic purposes

Self-inflicted injuries or attempted suicide

Treatment for infertility, sterility, or assisted reproduction

Dental treatment unless necessitated due to an accident

Experimental, unproven, or non-standard treatment procedures

Treatment primarily for psychiatric or mental disorders

7. Non-covered Conditions

The following are not covered under this policy:

Outpatient treatment not leading to hospitalization

Expenses incurred outside India

Treatment taken without proper medical advice or prescription

8. Claim Documentation Requirements

The insured must submit the following documents for claim processing:

Duly filled and signed claim form

Original hospital bills and receipts

Discharge summary and medical reports

Diagnostic test reports

Doctor’s prescription and consultation notes

Failure to submit complete documentation may result in delay or rejection of the claim.

9. Fraud and Misrepresentation

Any claim found to be fraudulent, supported by false documents, or involving misrepresentation of material facts shall be rejected, and the policy may be cancelled with immediate effect.

10. Claim Settlement Decision

The company reserves the right to approve, partially approve, or reject any claim based on:

Medical necessity

Policy terms and exclusions

Completeness of documentation

Compliance with waiting periods

11. Dispute Resolution

Any disputes arising out of this policy shall be subject to the jurisdiction of courts located in India.
